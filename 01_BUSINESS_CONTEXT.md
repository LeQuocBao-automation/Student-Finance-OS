# Student Finance OS — Business Context

## Business
Student Finance OS is a Vietnam-first one-person digital business focused on helping university students manage money in a practical, behavior-friendly way.

## Target customer
- Vietnamese university students
- Students with limited or irregular income
- Students who want to save but struggle with spending and planning
- Students who find traditional budgeting restrictive or difficult to maintain

## Core problem
Many students know they should save money, but traditional budgeting advice can feel restrictive, complicated, or unrealistic for student life.

The business aims to make money management easier to understand and easier to practice.

## Core philosophy: Save First
Saving is treated as a positive first action rather than something left over after spending.

The system is adaptive rather than based on a rigid daily spending limit.

## Core product concept
Student Money OS:

Income -> Save First -> Must Keep -> Flexible Spending -> Goals -> Review

## Current implementation direction
- Google Sheets
- Google Forms
- Apps Script where useful

## Free entry product
Student Money Checkup.

Purpose:
- give immediate value
- help students identify their current money-management situation
- introduce the Save First philosophy
- create a bridge toward the paid product

## Paid product
Student Money OS/System.

Initial pricing direction: roughly 49k–99k VND. This is a hypothesis to validate, not a permanent decision.

## Business model
Free useful entry point -> trust/problem awareness -> paid digital product -> feedback -> product improvement.

## Current distribution
Primary owned channel: Facebook Page.
Relevant Facebook groups may be used for genuine participation and adapted content distribution.

## Current stage
The Facebook Page was created recently. The business should prioritize awareness, relatable student-money content, understanding real student problems, early users, feedback, and first sales.

Do not prematurely behave like a large software company.
