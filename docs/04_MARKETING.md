# Student Finance OS — Marketing & Content Context

## Audience
Vietnamese university students.

## Tone
- friendly
- relatable
- practical
- clear
- student-native
- not preachy
- not overly corporate
- not financial-guru style

## Avoid
- fear-based financial messaging
- shame around spending
- unrealistic saving advice
- overly complex finance terminology
- aggressive selling before trust exists
- pretending the product solves every financial problem

## Content pillars
### 1. Relatable / Meme
Student money situations people recognize immediately.

### 2. Student Money Situations
Concrete situations such as allowance/income arriving, spending too quickly, food/transport/social spending, end-of-month shortage, irregular income, or saving for a goal.

### 3. Money Reframe / Insights
Simple ways to think differently about saving and spending.

### 4. Student Life / Stories
Realistic student experiences and situations.

### 5. Questions / Interaction
Posts designed to generate genuine conversation and learn what students experience.

## Current publishing direction
Target roughly 4–6 Page posts per week during early testing, with light group participation and distribution. This is a working cadence, not a rigid rule.

## Content workflow
Research/idea -> hook -> content -> visual/reel if appropriate -> human review -> publish -> observe reach/engagement/comments -> record useful insights -> improve future content.

## Future automation
Potentially automate:
- idea generation
- content drafting
- Reel scripts
- programmatic video creation
- scheduling
- publishing
- analytics summaries

Automation must preserve brand voice and should not publish risky or off-brand content without appropriate approval.
