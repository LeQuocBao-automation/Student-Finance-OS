# Student Finance OS — Strategy

## Current strategic objective
Validate that Vietnamese students recognize the money problems being addressed and are willing to use/pay for a practical Student Money OS.

## Month-1 frame
Build audience and problem awareness before aggressive selling.

Desired reactions:
- “This is exactly my situation.”
- “I have this money problem too.”
- “I never thought about money this way.”
- “Maybe I need a system.”

## Content pillars
1. Relatable / Meme
2. Student Money Situations
3. Money Reframe / Insights
4. Student Life / Stories
5. Questions / Interaction

Humorous and relatable visual content is useful, while still connecting to the broader student-money problem.

## Distribution
- Facebook Page is the main owned channel.
- Adapt suitable content for relevant Facebook groups.
- Comment genuinely on relevant discussions.
- Avoid spam and hard selling.

## Sales philosophy
Early priority is learning, not maximizing immediate sales. Learn:
- what students react to
- which problems feel urgent
- which messages create interest
- whether the product promise is understood
- what prevents purchase or use

## One-person business constraint
The owner has limited time. Prefer reusable templates, batch workflows, automation, AI assistance, and low operational overhead.

## Decision hierarchy
1. Real user evidence
2. Clear business objective
3. Simplicity
4. Automation
5. Technical sophistication

Automation is valuable when it removes repetitive work; automation itself is not the goal.
